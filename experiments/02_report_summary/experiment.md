# Long Report → Executive Summary

## Problem

Executives rarely have time to read long reports before meetings.

## Solution

Use AI to extract only the most decision-relevant information.

## Prompt

Summarize the following report for a busy executive.

Return:

Key insights (3)
Major risks (3)
Recommended actions (3)

Report:
{{report_text}}

## Result

A 35-page market report was reduced to a 1-page summary in under a minute.

## Learnings

Providing the role ("busy executive") improves prioritisation of insights.
